04 / ABOUT ELECNOVELLE

Engineering ideas into reliable products.

We are a team of hardware and embedded systems engineers focused on turning complex ideas into reliable, production-ready products. With $36M+ in project value delivered, 50+ international clients served, and 100+ projects shipped, we bring experience across high-speed, mixed-signal, RF, and power electronics.

Our expertise includes multilayer PCB design up to 16 layers, FPGA-based systems, embedded firmware, signal and power integrity (SI/PI), and end-to-end hardware development. From system architecture and schematic design to PCB layout, prototyping, hardware bring-up, validation, and production support, we deliver solutions tailored to real-world engineering challenges.

Our team combines practical R&D experience with research-driven innovation, including IEEE CIoT 2025 publication work. We are committed to engineering high-performance, reliable, and manufacturable electronic products for clients worldwide.
